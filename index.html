How to read it: the painting is a 24-hour clock (UTC), midnight at the top. Each half-hour the hamster runs becomes a stroke from the centre outward; more work makes it longer and heavier. Quiet half-hours leave bare canvas. Every day gets its own palette, seeded by the date. Today's settlements land as paint drops, placed and coloured by their transaction hash, so every one is unique and checkable on Sepolia.
